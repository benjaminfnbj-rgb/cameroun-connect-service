Our first product · Live

🏥 Santé Connect Cameroun

The digital health platform connecting patients, doctors, pharmacies and health facilities in Cameroon. Available on a phone, tablet or computer.

👩🏾‍⚕️

For patients

Find a doctor or health facility more easily, and get informed before travelling.

🩺

For doctors

Be visible online and stay in touch with patients.

💊

For pharmacies

Join a connected health network and be found more easily by patients.

🤖

Docta IA

Santé Connect Cameroun's smart assistant: it answers general health questions and points you to the right professionals.

Why it matters: in Cameroon, finding the right information or the right health professional often takes time. Santé Connect Cameroun brings patients closer to health professionals, everywhere in the country.

🏥 Open the application

Santé Connect Cameroun and Docta IA do not replace a medical consultation, diagnosis or treatment by a health professional. In an emergency, contact the emergency services immediately or go to the nearest health facility. Use of the application is governed by its own terms of use and privacy policy.
