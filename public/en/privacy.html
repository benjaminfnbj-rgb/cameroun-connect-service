Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: we only collect the data you enter in the contact form, in order to reply to you. We do not sell it. We use no advertising cookies and no tracking tools. You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Cameroun Connect Service ("CCS", "we"), a company governed by Cameroonian law, reachable at contact@comconnectservice.com.

This policy applies to the website www.comconnectservice.com (the "Website"). The Santé Connect Cameroun application and its assistant Docta IA have their own privacy policy, available at sante.comconnectservice.com.

2. Applicable law

We process your data in accordance with Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data in Cameroon, Law No. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybersecurity and cybercrime, and Law No. 2010/021 of 21 December 2010 governing electronic commerce. For people located in the European Union, we also apply Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

3. What data do we collect?

SituationDataPurposeLegal basis

Please do not send us health data or other sensitive data through the form. If you do so anyway, we will delete it as soon as possible.

We make no automated decisions producing legal effects concerning you, and we do not use your data for profiling or advertising.

4. Who can access your data?

Your data is accessible only to CCS staff authorised to handle your request. It may also be processed on our behalf and on our instructions by the following technical providers:

  • Vercel Inc. (United States): Website hosting;
  • Resend (United States): delivery of emails sent from the contact form;
  • our business email provider, for receiving and storing emails.

We do not sell or rent your data. We may disclose it to the authorities where required by law.

5. Transfers outside Cameroon

As our hosting and email providers are located in the United States, your data may be transferred there. These transfers are covered by the contractual data protection commitments of these providers, to ensure an appropriate level of protection in accordance with applicable regulations.

6. How long do we keep your data?

  • Contact form messages and data: 3 years from our last exchange, then deleted, unless a contractual relationship requires longer retention;
  • Technical connection data: a limited period set by our host for security reasons;
  • Data used to limit abusive submissions: a few minutes, in temporary memory.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data: encrypted connections (HTTPS), access limited to authorised people, protection against automated submissions, and providers that follow recognised security standards. As no system is perfectly secure, we will inform you and the competent authority of any data breach likely to put you at risk, as provided by law.

8. Your rights

Under applicable law, you have the following rights over your data:

  • right to information and right of access;
  • right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • right to erasure;
  • right to object and right to restriction of processing;
  • right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • for people located in the European Union: right to data portability.

To exercise your rights, write to contact@comconnectservice.com describing your request. We may ask you to prove your identity if we have reasonable doubts. We reply within one month at most.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Cameroonian personal data protection authority established by Law No. 2024/017 and, if you live in the European Union, with the data protection authority of your country.

9. Minors

The Website is intended for adults, in particular professionals, partners and institutions. We do not knowingly collect data about minors. If a minor has sent us data, their legal guardian may ask us to delete it.

10. Cookies

The Website uses no advertising cookies and no audience measurement tools. To learn more, see our cookie policy.

11. Changes

We may update this policy, in particular to reflect legal or technical changes. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page. We will clearly flag any significant change on the Website.

12. Language

This policy is available in French and English. This is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.
