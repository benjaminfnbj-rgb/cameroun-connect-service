Cookie policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: the Website sets no advertising cookies, no audience measurement cookies and no social media trackers. That is why no consent banner is shown.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. Similar technologies (browser local storage, pixels, identifiers) are called "trackers". Some are needed for a website to work; others are used to measure audiences or show advertising.

2. Cookies used on this Website

The website www.comconnectservice.com:

  • sets no advertising cookies;
  • uses no audience measurement tools (no Google Analytics, for example);
  • includes no social media buttons or plug-ins that would set trackers;
  • hosts its own fonts: no connection is made to third-party servers such as Google Fonts during your visit.

The contact form works without cookies. Our host may use mechanisms that are strictly necessary for security and page delivery (for example protection against attacks). These mechanisms are not used to track you and do not require your consent.

3. Linked websites

When you follow a link to another website, including the Santé Connect Cameroun application (sante.comconnectservice.com), that website may use its own cookies, described in its own policy.

4. Changes to this policy

If we ever add non-essential cookies (audience measurement, for example), we will first ask for your consent through a banner. You will be able to accept, refuse or customise your choice just as easily, and change it at any time. This page will be updated accordingly.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

You can view, block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.), usually under "Privacy" or "Cookies". Blocking all cookies may prevent some websites from working properly.
